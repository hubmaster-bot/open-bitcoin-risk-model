# OBRM v1.1.0 — Dashboard UX and Chart Annotations

OBRM v1.1.0 improves day-to-day chart research without changing Composite Risk v1.

## Highlights

- Right-side Research Inspector with historical date selection, model dimensions, weights, contributions, and explanations.
- Persistent vertical date lines for events and personal milestones.
- Persistent horizontal BTC price and Composite Risk levels.
- Labels and line styles, individual deletion, clear-all, and JSON/CSV export.
- Local annotation storage under `data/user/chart_annotations.json`.
- `yfinance==1.4.1` pin to prevent the Windows startup regression seen with yfinance 1.5.1.

## Model governance

Composite Risk methodology, weights, thresholds, decisions, and evidence promotion status are unchanged from v1.0.0.
